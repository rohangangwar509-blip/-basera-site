How to request deletion

Email privacy.basera@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject line Delete my account.

What to include

  • The email address you sign in with.
  • The name of your organization in Basera, if you have one.
  • Whether you want only your own login removed, or the organization's records as well.

How your request is handled

Requests are checked and processed by a person; deletion is not automatic and does not happen the moment you send the email. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before we act — that is to stop somebody else deleting your data, not to delay you.
