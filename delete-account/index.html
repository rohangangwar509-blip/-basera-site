Basera — paying-guest and rent management

Delete your Basera account

Dropixelx operates Basera: PG & Rent Manager. Basera is used by property owners and managers to run paying-guest accommodation. This page is how you ask us to delete your account and the data held under it. You do not need the app installed, and you do not need to sign in.

How to request deletion

Email privacy.basera@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject line Delete my account.

What to include

  • The email address you sign in with.
  • The name of your organization in Basera, if you have one.
  • Whether you want only your own login removed, or the organization's records as well.

How your request is handled

Requests are checked and processed by a person; deletion is not automatic and does not happen the moment you send the email. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before we act — that is to stop somebody else deleting your data, not to delay you.

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and complete it within 30 days, subject to the lawful retention described below.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity: your email address, and your Google sign-in link if you used one.
  • Your user profile: your name, phone number and profile photo.

Deleting your login and profile does not automatically delete your organization’s business records — properties, tenants, agreements, invoices and ledger entries. Those are deleted only if you ask for the whole organization to be deleted, which is a separate, explicit request.

What may be kept, and why

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and tenancy records, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations. When the data is no longer required for these purposes, we delete or anonymize it, subject to any lawful retention requirement.

In practice that means records of rent, deposits and receipts are normally kept while a tenancy, a balance or a dispute is still live, and for as long afterwards as is reasonably necessary for the purposes above.

If you manage an organization with other members, deleting your own account does not delete theirs either. To have the whole organization deleted, say so explicitly in your email; we will confirm with you before acting, because it destroys records other members may still depend on.

Residents and visitors

If you are a resident or a visitor whose details were entered into Basera by a property manager, we hold that data on their behalf rather than our own. Residents should contact the operator first. If the operator does not respond, they may contact privacy.basera@gmail.com and Basera will assist with the request, but will not override the operator’s records without appropriate authority.
