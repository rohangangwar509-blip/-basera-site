Dropixelx operates Basera: PG & Rent Manager. Basera (“we”) is an application for managing paying-guest accommodation, hostels and co-living properties. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

“Dropixelx” is the name Basera is published under on Google Play. Basera is operated by Rohan Gangwar, an individual developer, through a personal Google Play developer account. Dropixelx is a brand name, not a separate legal person.

Who this policy is for

Basera is used by property operators and their staff. Two groups appear in it:

  • Users — the staff who sign in: owners, managers, reception, accountants and other roles.
  • Residents and visitors — people whose details a user records. They do not sign in.

Residents should contact the operator first. If the operator does not respond, they may contact privacy.basera@gmail.com and Basera will assist with the request, but will not override the operator’s records without appropriate authority.

What we collect

Account information (users)

Name, email address, and a profile photo URL if you sign in with Google. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; we never see or store your password.

Resident information (entered by users)

Name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, current and permanent address, occupation, emergency contact details.

Government identity documents. The type of document (Aadhaar, PAN or other) and the last four digits are stored on the resident record. The full number is stored separately and is readable only by users holding the “manage tenants” permission. Scanned copies, where uploaded, are stored as file attachments under the same restriction.

Visitor information (entered by users)

Name, phone number, who they are visiting, arrival and departure times, vehicle number and notes.

The app no longer collects visitors’ identity-document numbers. Those fields were removed from visitor check-in. Visitor records created before that change may still contain an identity-document type and number, and those existing values remain in the database.

Property and financial records

Property, building, floor, room and bed details; tenancy agreements including rent, deposit and maintenance charges; invoices, payment receipts and running balances; the operator’s own expenses.

We do not collect or process payment instruments. No card, bank account or UPI credential is collected, stored or transmitted. Payments are recorded as having taken place; they are never processed through this app.

Files

Photographs, scanned documents and other files uploaded as attachments to complaints, maintenance tickets, tenancy agreements or resident records.

Technical information

Crash reports and diagnostic data via Firebase Crashlytics, to find and fix defects. We do not collect location, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or an advertising identifier, and the app contains no analytics or advertising software.

Why we collect it

We process personal data to provide and administer Basera, maintain accommodation and financial records, provide support, protect the security and integrity of the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required, we obtain it.

Who can see it

Data is scoped to the organization that entered it. Access within an organization is governed by role: not every member can see resident identity documents, financial records or the audit log.

We do not sell data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Our primary infrastructure is Supabase, which provides the database, authentication and file storage, and processes data on our behalf under its terms. We also use Google Firebase for two limited purposes only: Crashlytics (crash and error reports) and App Check (verifying that requests come from a genuine copy of the app). Firebase is not the data store.

Data region: AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai).

How long we keep it

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and tenancy records, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations. When the data is no longer required for these purposes, we delete or anonymize it, subject to any lawful retention requirement.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion from inside the app, under Settings → Account → Delete account, or directly at our account deletion page. Requests are checked and processed by a person; deletion is not automatic. We acknowledge a request within 3 business days and complete it within 30 days, subject to the lawful retention set out above.

Deleting your own login does not by itself delete your organization’s business records — properties, tenants, agreements, invoices and ledger entries. Deleting a whole organization is a separate, explicit request.

When you request deletion we delete your sign-in identity and your user profile. Your organization’s business records are not deleted unless you separately ask for the whole organization to be deleted. Records we keep are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes set out under How long we keep it above, and are then deleted or anonymized, subject to any lawful retention requirement.

Your rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion/erasure where applicable, and grievance redressal. Requests will normally be addressed within 30 days, subject to applicable law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is controlled by server-side authorization rules rather than by the app alone. Application data is excluded from Android’s automatic device backup, so it is not copied to personal cloud storage.

Children

Basera is not directed to children. Operators may record information about residents under 18 where legally permitted and are responsible for obtaining any consent or authorization required by applicable law.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the Last updated date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the app.
